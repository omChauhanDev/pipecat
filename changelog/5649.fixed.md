- Fixed `DeepgramFluxSTTService` and `DeepgramFluxSageMakerSTTService` silently dropping a settings update made while the connection is down. Those fields are normally sent over the live connection, so with no connection there was nothing to send them over and nothing reconnected, yet the service was marked usable again and went on discarding audio. Correcting the setting that broke the connection now reconnects and applies it, since Flux reads all of those fields from the connection URL too.
